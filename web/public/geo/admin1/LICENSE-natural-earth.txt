admin1/<ISO2>.json: first-level regions (states, provinces) per country, cut from Natural Earth
"ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces" (https://www.naturalearthdata.com, https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector),
2026-10-28, simplified (Douglas-Peucker, about 0.004 degrees, more for the very large countries so a file stays
under 150 KB) by scripts/geo/build-admin1.mjs. Run it again with the source GeoJSON to rebuild.

We credit it anyway in the region map footnote ("Region shapes: Natural Earth (public domain)").
Differences from Natural Earth: Great Britain is merged into England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, and
France, Spain and Italy into their regions, because that is the level Google Analytics reports. Region names in
other languages are left out; the English name and a few alternative spellings are kept so Google's names match.
