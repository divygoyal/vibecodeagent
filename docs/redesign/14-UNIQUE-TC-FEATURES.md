## New Features to Add That Rybbit Doesn't Have

These are features that would make TrafficClaw categorically better than Rybbit:

### 1. AI Insights on Every Tab
**What:** Automatic AI-generated insight card on each analytics page. E.g., on the Retention page: "Your Day-7 retention improved 15% this month — likely due to the new onboarding flow launched on Mar 15."
**Why:** No analytics tool explains WHY metrics changed. We already have the AI — just connect it per-page.
**Effort:** Medium (2-3 hrs)

### 2. Anomaly Detection
**What:** ML-based anomaly detection that flags unusual patterns BEFORE the user notices. "Traffic from Germany dropped 40% today — investigating..."
**Why:** Proactive > reactive. Users shouldn't have to stare at dashboards.
**Effort:** Medium (use simple z-score detection on historical data)

### 3. Competitor Tracking
**What:** Monitor competitor rankings and estimated traffic. Compare your keywords vs competitors.
**Why:** Currently requires SEMrush ($100+/mo). We can offer basic competitor intel for free.
**Effort:** High (needs third-party data source)

### 4. Content Calendar
**What:** Plan and schedule content based on keyword opportunities. "Publish about [keyword] by [date] to capture [X] monthly searches."
**Why:** Connects SEO insights to action. No analytics tool does this.
**Effort:** Medium (2-3 hrs for basic calendar)

### 5. Rank Tracker
**What:** Daily keyword position tracking with history charts. See how each keyword moves over time.
**Why:** Currently requires Ahrefs/SEMrush. We already have GSC data.
**Effort:** Medium (use GSC historical data, store daily snapshots)

### 6. White-Label Reports
**What:** Custom-branded PDF reports for agencies. Upload client logo, customize colors, add agency branding.
**Why:** Agencies are a huge market. They'll pay for white-label.
**Effort:** Medium (extend PDF export with branding options)

### 7. Webhook Integrations
**What:** Send alerts to Slack, Discord, email, or custom webhook URLs.
**Why:** Teams live in Slack/Discord. Alerts should go where they already are.
**Effort:** Low (1-2 hrs, just HTTP POST on alert trigger)

### 8. AI Chat in Every Tab
**What:** A contextual mini-chat on each analytics page. On the Performance page, it knows you're looking at CWV. Ask: "How can I improve my LCP?" and it answers with specific advice for YOUR site.
**Why:** Context-aware AI is 10x more useful than a generic chatbot.
**Effort:** Low (reuse existing AI chat, inject page context into system prompt)

### 9. Smart Annotations
**What:** Auto-detect events that correlate with metric changes and annotate them on charts. "Google algorithm update detected" or "New blog post published" shown as markers on the traffic chart.
**Why:** Explains WHY graphs changed. Google Search Status dashboard + your deploy history.
**Effort:** High (needs event correlation engine)

### 10. SEO Score Leaderboard
**What:** Public leaderboard showing SEO scores of opted-in sites. Gamification for SEO improvement.
**Why:** Already partially built (leaderboard exists). Just needs polish.
**Effort:** Low (already exists, just promote it)
